Exterior Painting FAQs

How much does exterior house painting cost near me?

Exterior house painting in Hudson, MA typically ranges from $3,000-$8,000 for a standard home depending on size, siding type, and condition. Multi-story homes and larger properties may cost more. We provide free, detailed estimates.

What is the best time to paint a house exterior in Massachusetts?

The ideal exterior painting season in Massachusetts is late spring through early fall (May-October) when temperatures are consistently above 50°F. We monitor weather conditions closely to ensure optimal paint application and curing.

How long does exterior paint last in New England?

With proper preparation and quality paint, exterior paint typically lasts 10-15 years in New England. Our thorough prep work and premium paints help maximize longevity despite our harsh weather conditions.

Do you paint in cold weather?

We typically pause exterior painting when temperatures drop below 50°F, as paint needs proper conditions to cure correctly. We schedule projects to ensure the best results for New England's climate.
